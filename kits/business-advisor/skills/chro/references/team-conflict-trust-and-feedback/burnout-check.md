# Burnout check for a team

Use when a team is tired, cynical or slipping, before treating it as a conflict or motivation problem. Synthesised from published research on burnout; wording ours.

## Recognise it
Three signs together: **exhaustion** (drained, not recovered by a weekend), **cynicism** (detached, distrustful, "why bother"), **reduced effectiveness** (less done, lower quality). Burnout is a work-design problem, not a personal weakness; fixing the individual alone rarely works.

## Find the cause: six sources
Score each 1 (fine) to 5 (serious) with the team, anonymously if trust is low:
1. **Workload**: unsustainable volume or pace.
2. **Control**: little say over how, when or what.
3. **Reward**: effort not recognised, in pay or appreciation.
4. **Community**: isolation, poor support from colleagues.
5. **Fairness**: favouritism, unequal workloads, opaque decisions.
6. **Values and skills fit**: work clashes with what people value or are good at.
Tackle the highest two first.

## Protective factors to build
- **Manageable workload**: one of the strongest protections. Say clearly what the priorities are and what can wait or stop.
- **Purpose**: people who see why their work matters burn out less (do not use purpose to excuse overload).
- **Able to talk about strain**: psychological safety, so people can raise mental health or overload without penalty; respond to mistakes with "what help do you need to get back on track?" rather than blame.
- **Connection**: regular contact with colleagues and the manager.

## Quick fixes the manager controls
- **Meeting filter**: Is this meeting necessary? If yes: does it need video? Must it exceed 30 minutes? Who is essential? Can some be audio-only? Start with a short check-in and let people with back-to-back calls leave early.
- **Check in often**: "How are you doing?" and "How can I help?" asked individually, not only in the team meeting.
- **Support in place before a crisis**: a list of support services; flexible or reduced hours and leave policies for health or caring needs; a few people trained to notice strain and point colleagues to help.
- Re-run the six-source score after a month to see if it moved.
