# Chief people officer: teams, leadership, change, influence and people analytics

**Outcome:** one owner for every business method this role needs. Each area below is a full method (outcome, when to use, method, worked pattern, common mistakes, sources, gaps) kept in its own reference file.

## How to use

1. Find the area or areas that match the task with "Which area when", then confirm in the table below.
2. Open each matching file and follow its method. Paths are relative to the module's `skills/` folder.
3. A name in backticks that is not in this table belongs to another role; the list at the end says which, and its file is `<role>/references/<name>.md`. Work that spans roles starts from `chief-of-staff/SKILL.md`.
4. Say which area the method came from.

## Which area when

**The usual questions**

- "We are forming a project team, task force or new department" → `team-launch-and-charter`
- "The team is fighting, going quiet, carrying a free rider or burning out" → `team-conflict-trust-and-feedback`
- "We agree too fast; why did that decision go wrong?" → `group-decision-making`
- "How do I manage this team or re-engage this employee? What is my leadership style?" → `leading-and-motivating-people`
- "We are rolling out a new system, process or restructure and people resist" → `leading-change`
- "I need buy-in from people I don't control; how do I handle my boss or the politics?" → `power-and-influence`
- "How should we hire, rate staff, write the survey, or buy this HR or monitoring tool?" → `people-analytics`

**Look-alikes, told apart**

- Team areas work on a group: `team-launch-and-charter` forms it, `team-conflict-trust-and-feedback` keeps it healthy, `group-decision-making` makes its choices sound.
- Leading areas work through people: `leading-and-motivating-people` is a manager and their people; `leading-change` is an organisation adopting something new; `power-and-influence` is getting things done without authority.
- `people-analytics` measures people (hiring, ratings, surveys, tools) and tests the measures; it does not lead them. General data method is the CDO's `analytics-for-decisions`; pay design against hidden effort is the CEO's `incentives-and-information`; a salary negotiation is the CEO's `negotiation-deal-making`.

**Often used together**

- Reorganise: `power-and-influence` (map who decides) → `leading-change` → `team-launch-and-charter` for the new teams → the CMO's `strategic-communication-plan`.
- Hire and measure: `people-analytics` (structured interview, validity) → `leading-and-motivating-people` (role design) → the CDO's `data-visualisation-and-dashboards` for the scorecard.
- Fix a struggling team: `team-conflict-trust-and-feedback` → `group-decision-making` → `team-launch-and-charter` to relaunch.

## Areas

| Area | Use when | File |
|---|---|---|
| `team-launch-and-charter` | Design a new team and launch it with a written charter (purpose, goals, roles, rules of engagement, task plan, feedback loop) so it performs sooner and longer; or relaunch one that is drifting. | `chro/references/team-launch-and-charter.md` |
| `team-conflict-trust-and-feedback` | Keep a working team healthy by diagnosing and resolving conflict, building trust and psychological safety, handling free riders, managing team mood and stress, making sure the right people get heard and credited, and running feedback and midpoint reviews. | `chro/references/team-conflict-trust-and-feedback.md` |
| `group-decision-making` | Help a leadership team or working group make better decisions by spotting individual biases and group failure modes (groupthink, common-information bias, cascades, sunk cost, halo and rater effects) and applying structural fixes. | `chro/references/group-decision-making.md` |
| `leading-and-motivating-people` | Help an owner or manager lead people well: a leadership approach that fits the situation, the right kind of power, re-engaging unmotivated staff through role design and reinforcement, and a personal leadership purpose. | `chro/references/leading-and-motivating-people.md` |
| `leading-change` | Plan and run an organisational change (new system, process, policy, restructure or culture shift) so people adopt it, using the change formula, adoption stages and a sequenced set of change levers. | `chro/references/leading-change.md` |
| `power-and-influence` | Get things done inside an organisation or across partners without relying on formal authority, by diagnosing the politics of a situation, auditing your power bases, and building an influence plan with coalitions, persuasion principles, a sequence and checkpoints; also mapping networks, managing up, holding the ethical line, learning from case lessons on how power is gained and lost, and building a reputation that carries power. | `chro/references/power-and-influence.md` |
| `people-analytics` | Build, check or challenge a measure of people (hiring scores, structured vs unstructured interviews, AI screening, performance ratings, engagement surveys and question design, sentiment, monitoring or biometric tools) for validity, reliability, rater and method effects, privacy, dignity and gaming, and show whether it moves business results, before staffing, pay or policy decisions rest on it. | `chro/references/people-analytics.md` |
