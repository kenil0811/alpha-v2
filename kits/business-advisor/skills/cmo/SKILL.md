# Chief marketing officer: markets, customers, brand, channels, pricing and communication

**Outcome:** one owner for every business method this role needs. Each area below is a full method (outcome, when to use, method, worked pattern, common mistakes, sources, gaps) kept in its own reference file.

## How to use

1. Find the area or areas that match the task with "Which area when", then confirm in the table below.
2. Open each matching file and follow its method. Paths are relative to the module's `skills/` folder.
3. A name in backticks that is not in this table belongs to another role; the list at the end says which, and its file is `<role>/references/<name>.md`. Work that spans roles starts from `chief-of-staff/SKILL.md`.
4. Say which area the method came from.

## Which area when

**The usual questions**

- "We are launching: who first, in what order, how fast? How do we enter this country?" → `market-entry-and-go-to-market` (it covers home launches too)
- "How big is this market? Who do we target first? Does each sale make money? Revenue for the plan?" → `market-sizing-and-unit-economics`
- "What should we charge? Raise or cut? Discount, membership or bundle?" → `pricing-strategy`
- "Why don't customers buy? How do we design the offer or sign-up flow?" → `consumer-behavior-marketing`
- "We look like everyone else. What do we stand for?" → `brand-and-positioning`
- "Where should the marketing budget go? Why isn't the website converting? Is this campaign paying back?" → `digital-marketing-and-channels`
- "How many will we sell in year 3, and when do sales peak? Is this promotion worth it?" → `new-product-forecasting`
- "Is this a good idea? What do we test first? Persevere, pivot or stop?" → `opportunity-validation`
- "We are announcing something and need one story for staff, investors, customers and press" → `strategic-communication-plan`
- "Something has gone wrong in public" → `crisis-communication`
- "Turn this analysis into a deck or talk that wins the decision" → `business-storytelling`

**Look-alikes, told apart**

- `market-sizing-and-unit-economics` sizes the prize and checks each sale pays; `new-product-forecasting` shapes adoption over time (Bass) and measures promotion and ad response; `opportunity-validation` asks whether to do it at all, with cheap tests. The COO's `demand-forecast-distributions` is the forecast with a spread for orders and stock.
- `brand-and-positioning` is what the brand stands for against rivals; `consumer-behavior-marketing` is how people notice, feel and choose, applied to offers and flows.
- `strategic-communication-plan` is planned, for all stakeholders; `crisis-communication` is after something broke; `business-storytelling` is one deck or talk to win one decision. The CEO's `business-plan-and-pitch` is the venture's own investor documents.
- `market-entry-and-go-to-market` is the launch plan and entry mode; `digital-marketing-and-channels` runs the channels inside it; `pricing-strategy` sets its price.
- `pricing-strategy` sets the price from demand; the CFO's `product-costing` gives the cost floor; the CEO's `competitive-moves` predicts the rival's answer.

**Often used together**

- Launch a product: `opportunity-validation` → `market-sizing-and-unit-economics` → `brand-and-positioning` → `pricing-strategy` → `market-entry-and-go-to-market` → `digital-marketing-and-channels` → `new-product-forecasting`.
- Enter a country: `market-entry-and-go-to-market` → `market-sizing-and-unit-economics` → the COO's `risk-and-compliance` → the CFO's `capital-budgeting`.
- Recommend and win: `market-sizing-and-unit-economics` or the analysis area → `business-storytelling` → `strategic-communication-plan`.

## Areas

| Area | Use when | File |
|---|---|---|
| `market-entry-and-go-to-market` | Market entry and go-to-market: plan a launch as one system (target, brand, position, price, place, properties, media, trust, metrics), pick the beachhead and the order of later segments, judge timing and first-mover claims; for a new country, measure distance, score entry modes, own versus contract, and stage with gates and kill criteria. | `cmo/references/market-entry-and-go-to-market.md` |
| `market-sizing-and-unit-economics` | Size a market (top-down, bottom-up, TAM/SAM/SOM, by scenario of need), segment it and choose the launch segment (fit, readiness, resistance), check that each sale makes money (margins, break-even, CAC, lifetime value, credit-financed and durable-goods customers, B2B funnel), track churn, retention and customer equity, and build a 3 to 5 year revenue forecast. | `cmo/references/market-sizing-and-unit-economics.md` |
| `pricing-strategy` | Set and structure prices from customer demand - elasticity, the profit-maximising price, segment prices, versions, two-part tariffs, bundles, yield controls - price a new product for launch (channel waterfall, goals, CAC and lifetime value) and judge promotions. | `cmo/references/pricing-strategy.md` |
| `consumer-behavior-marketing` | Understand why customers buy and design marketing (offers, menus, ads, stores, sign-up flows) that works with how people actually notice, remember, feel and choose. | `cmo/references/consumer-behavior-marketing.md` |
| `brand-and-positioning` | Build a brand foundation (vision, mission, values, personality, proof) and a position that owns a functional and emotional space: positioning statement, brand essence, perceptual maps and competitor sets, and testing where a new concept lands. | `cmo/references/brand-and-positioning.md` |
| `digital-marketing-and-channels` | Plan the growth engine across paid, owned and earned media: intent and journey maps, targeting, return targets from margin, search, social, email, influencers and PR, websites that convert, campaigns, attribution and funnel metrics (ROAS, CPC, conversion, lead scores, NPS). | `cmo/references/digital-marketing-and-channels.md` |
| `new-product-forecasting` | Forecast adoption of a new product (Bass and generalized Bass: peak timing, weighted analogues, price and ad effects, replacements), split category sales among brands (choice shares, word of mouth), short-run smoothing, and measure promotion and advertising response (Scan*Pro and its decomposition, carryover, Adstock, media mix). | `cmo/references/new-product-forecasting.md` |
| `opportunity-validation` | Find and test a new venture, product line or service before money is spent: customer pain first, hypotheses, business-model design on one page, value proposition, customer discovery, MVP and falsifiable tests, and the call to persevere, pivot or stop. | `cmo/references/opportunity-validation.md` |
| `strategic-communication-plan` | Build a company's strategic communication plan (strategy, vision-mission-values, stakeholder map, SWOT, message map) and a launch campaign with talking points and stakeholder Q&A. | `cmo/references/strategic-communication-plan.md` |
| `crisis-communication` | Prepare for and respond to a business crisis: a pre-crisis plan with risk brainstorm and pre-drafted statements, first responses using the CAP model, social and employee messages in the right order, a press statement with Q&A, and an apology that repairs trust. | `cmo/references/crisis-communication.md` |
| `business-storytelling` | Turn data and findings into a business story that wins a decision: situation-complication-resolution structure, a win theme, insight-to-action slides, short pitches, and the questioning and listening that find the story. | `cmo/references/business-storytelling.md` |
