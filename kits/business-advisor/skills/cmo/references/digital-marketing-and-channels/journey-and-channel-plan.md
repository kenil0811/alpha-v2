# Journey map and channel plan

Part of `cmo/references/digital-marketing-and-channels.md`.

## Growth-engine journey map (one per priority segment)
| Stage | Audience state | Objective | Core message or content | Channels and contexts | Success metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Passive → aware | Not looking; has the latent need | Make them care and notice | The tension, opened with the problem ("open with the fire") | Creators, PR, short video, contextual placements | Reach in segment, recall, branded search |
| Researching | Learning about the problem | Teach, earn credibility | How-to guides, tools, original research | SEO pages, YouTube, podcasts, newsletters | Engaged visits, time on page, sign-ups |
| Considering | Comparing options | Show difference and proof | Comparisons, reviews, demos, guarantees | Retargeting, reviews, marketplace listing, webinars | Return visits, demo or trial requests |
| Deciding | Ready to act | Remove friction | Offer, clear call to action, social proof | Paid search, landing page, sales call, retail | Conversion rate, cost per acquisition |
| Customer | Using it | Deliver early value | Onboarding, tips, community | Email, app, service | Activation, repeat, churn |
| Advocate | Satisfied | Make sharing easy and rewarding | Referral offer, review prompt, user content | Referral programme, social, community | Referrals, NPS, reviews |
Keep it to the logic of movement between stages, not 50 tactics.

## Per-touchpoint questions (why-where-what-how)
- **Why** is the customer in this channel (motivation: entertainment, learning, connection, solving a problem)?
- **Where** exactly (platform, publication, store, event)?
- **What** does success look like for them there, and for us?
- **How** do we fit the moment (format, length, tone, native to the platform)?

## Channel and tactic grid
| Goal | Paid | Owned | Earned |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Social video, display, sponsorship, celebrity | Content hub, SEO | PR, unpaid creators, word of mouth |
| Engagement | Retargeting, paid social | Email, site tools, webinars, social accounts | Reviews, community discussion |

## Situation analysis checklist 
Customers (behaviour, devices, platforms, needs); marketplace (search intermediaries, publishers, influencers, comparison sites, destination sites); competitors (including indirect and their digital capability); macro forces (privacy law, platform rules, economy, social trends such as digital minimalism); internal capability (a digital SWOT). Plan with SOSTAC: situation, objectives, strategy, tactics, actions, control; 12 to 18 months, revisited as results arrive.

## Forms of digital presence
Transactional (sells online), services and lead-building (informs, generates leads for offline sale: complex or high-end products), brand-building (experience only: low-value, high-volume goods), publisher or intermediary (earns from ads, commissions), community (customer-to-customer). Pick the form before the site plan.

## Martech rule
Technology serves the strategy, never leads it. Before adopting a tool: does it integrate with the rest, give consistent data, track the journey across channels, and earn a return? Train people at rollout.
