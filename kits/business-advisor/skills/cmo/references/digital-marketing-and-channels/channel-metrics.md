# Channel and funnel metrics

Part of `cmo/references/digital-marketing-and-channels.md`. Customer growth, churn and lifetime value live in `cmo/references/market-sizing-and-unit-economics/growth-metrics.md`.

## Formulas
| Metric | Formula | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | Ad cost ÷ impressions × 1,000 | Price of reach |
| CTR | Clicks ÷ impressions | Relevance of creative and targeting |
| CPC | Ad cost ÷ clicks | Price of a visit |
| Conversion rate | Conversions ÷ visits (or clicks) | Landing page and offer quality |
| Cost per acquisition | Ad cost ÷ conversions | Compare with allowable CAC |
| ROAS | Revenue from ads ÷ ad cost | Not profit |
| Break-even ROAS | 1 ÷ gross margin % | Below this, ads lose money on the first sale |
| Ad profit | Revenue × gross margin % − ad cost | The number that matters |
| Bounce rate | Single-page visits ÷ visits | Mismatch between promise and page, or slow page |
| Revenue per visit | Revenue ÷ visits, by source | Quality of traffic by channel |
| Email open / click rate | Opens (or unique clickers) ÷ delivered | Subject line / content relevance |
| List engagement | Share of list opening or clicking regularly | Health of the house list |
| Organic engagement rate | Interactions ÷ impressions (or followers) | Drives algorithmic reach |
| Share of search | Brand's searches ÷ category's branded searches | Leading indicator of market share |
| NPS | % promoters (9-10) − % detractors (0-6) | Advocacy |
| Online revenue contribution | Online sales ÷ total sales | Digital's direct share |

## A paid funnel worked through (own numbers)
Non-branded search: 200,000 impressions, CTR 2% → 4,000 clicks at $1.50 = $6,000. 25% start an application (1,000), 40% complete (400). Cost per completed application = $15. If 30% of completed applications become customers worth $120 gross profit each: 120 customers, $14,400 gross profit against $6,000 spend. Then check which step leaks most: improving completion from 40% to 50% adds as much as 25% more traffic, for free.

## Leading versus lagging
| Leading (act early) | Lagging (judge results) |
|---|---|
| Share of search, branded search | Revenue and profit against target |
| Engagement and return visits | Cost per acquisition |
| Trial and demo requests | Conversion efficiency |
| Satisfaction, NPS | Churn, repeat rate |

## Traffic sources
Source = where the visitor came from (a site), medium = how (organic, paid click, email, referral), channel = groups of sources by medium. Direct traffic includes untagged email clicks and app links, so tag everything.

## Attribution
Last-click gives all credit to the final referrer; first-click to the first; assisted or position-based models share credit. Use at least an assist report before cutting a discovery channel. For channels that resist measurement (podcasts, PR, word of mouth), track lift in branded search and direct traffic, run on-off tests by region or period, and ask "how did you hear about us?" at checkout (standard practice).

## Lead grading and scoring (B2B)
- **Grade (fit, A to D):** industry, location, company size, maturity, experience with the service, role of contact. Some rules move a grade directly (for example, in-territory adds one grade).
- **Score (behaviour, 0 to 10):** visits to pricing or case-study pages, downloads, webinar sign-ups, email clicks; decay to 0 after long inactivity.
- **Routing:** A or B with a high score → sales; good grade, low score → marketing nurture; poor grade → light-touch or none. Agree definitions of marketing-qualified and sales-qualified leads and send unready sales leads back to nurture.
- Track forecast win rate and revenue by grade-score cell to tune the rules.

## Dashboard (one page)
One row per funnel gear (discovery, engagement, conversion, loyalty, advocacy), with one leading and one lagging metric each, this period versus target and last period, and spend per gear. Add funnel velocity (days from lead to customer) and churn trend. Review monthly; change one lever at a time and note it.
