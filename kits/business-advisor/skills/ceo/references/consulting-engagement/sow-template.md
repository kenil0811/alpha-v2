# Statement of work: layout and quality bar

## Header
Date, client, project name, requested by, prepared by.

## Sections
1. **Executive summary** (1-2 paragraphs): the client's problem and why it matters; the expected
   impact; why the project is strategically important.
2. **Key objectives:** what the client will be able to decide or do at the end (for example,
   "make a go/no-go decision on entering the senior wellness market"). Not tasks.
3. **Phases, tasks and subtasks:** 3-5 phases that mirror the arc (discovery, analysis, solution
   development, final deliverable). For each task:
   - Summary, stated as an outcome ("develop customer insights from 15 interviews").
   - 2-4 key subtasks (not trivia like "send invites").
   - Outputs: the tangible asset (dataset, competitor map, financial model draft).
   - Dependencies: client data, interviews, approvals; what must finish first.
   - At least one review or synthesis checkpoint per phase.
   - Drop any task that does not advance an objective.
4. **Term, timeline and milestones:** start and end dates; when phases and hand-offs happen;
   only the moments that matter (kick-off, mid-point, draft, final).
5. **Deliverables:** tangible, measurable outputs, each tied to one or more objectives.
6. **Roles and responsibilities:** team, client lead, advisers, third parties.
7. **Communication plan:** kick-off, recurring check-ins, mid-point, final; channels (in person,
   virtual); how feedback is gathered; no surprises at the end.
8. **Change management:** how changes to scope, schedule or deliverables are requested and approved.
9. **Place of work** and logistics.
10. **Acceptance:** anything not listed is out of scope; signatures and dates from team lead,
    client lead and adviser. If a signature is hard to get, record who approved and when.

## Quality bar (suggested weights)
| Area | Weight | Excellent looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity and structure | 15% | Distinct sections, concise, easy to follow |
| Objectives | 20% | Specific, measurable, aligned with client need |
| Scope | 20% | Realistic, in and out of scope stated, dependencies named |
| Deliverables and milestones | 20% | Specific, actionable, tied to objectives with dates |
| Communication plan | 10% | Realistic rhythm and methods |
| Alignment with client | 15% | Shows understanding of stakeholder goals and nuances |

## Managing it
Position the SOW as a shared roadmap the client helped write, in plain language, that can be
adjusted together. Update the client proactively on progress and problems; when scope moves,
revise the SOW and mark the changes.
