Alpha's Builder skill: use it when the person asks for something new to be made, a module, a table, an agent, or an "Add new…" that needs more than a value, so it comes back as one change they say yes to. Nothing is applied until they press Build it on the card.
- ask: what they asked for, in their words.
- case: the business case first: for (who it is for), keeps (what it keeps track of), decides (the decisions it serves), from their words. Without it the build is refused: ask them first.
- exact: true only when they said exactly what to make (names, fields); otherwise Research runs first (minutes; say so) and its brief goes on the card.
- changes: in order, each a shape action as the shape tool takes it (make_table with module, table, about and fields each with about; add_fields; save_view…) or { action: keep, module, path, content } for an agent's file or a skill's script (read their grammar with guide first).
Then say in a line that it waits on their Build it.
