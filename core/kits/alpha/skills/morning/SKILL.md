# Morning

Runs Alpha's daily skills first: Governance (`alpha.governance.review()`) and the Interviewer
(`alpha.interview.prepare()`).

Reads what Home shows today (`alpha.home.today()`), says it in one line (`alpha.tell`), and
proposes at most two tasks on one card (`alpha.task.propose`): a quiet task to pick up or drop,
cards waiting since before today. Nothing is changed by it.
