# Research

A workspace skill of Alpha's. Given `alpha.input.ask` (and `about`, what is known of the
business), `alpha.research({ ask, about })` searches and reads within the core's bounds (searches,
pages, minutes in limits.yaml), never signing in or sending, and returns a short brief:
competitors and new startups, open-source alternatives with licence and activity (learn from or
reuse), and common practice, ranked by impact × reach ÷ effort. A line stays only when a source it
cites resolves. Alpha's desk calls it as `research`; any module's agent as `alpha.research`.
