# Interviewer

A skill of Alpha's, not a character: the person sees Alpha asking. `alpha.interview.prepare()`
refreshes one pool of questions: everything unresolved (open questions, failed agents, rules
waiting, empty, blank or stale tables) and a standing bank from the onboarding research (goals,
roles and tools, steps, exceptions, gaps, numbers, for the business and each module). Code values
each by what it unblocks, how much it helps, its age and its skips, and the model words the most
valuable. Interview me asks the most useful first and stops when the next is worth too little, or
the person is done or answers briefly. Morning runs it each day.
