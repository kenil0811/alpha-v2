# Network

Who the person knows, in circles they name. The people themselves are Alpha's own (view `people`:
from Contacts, the people they write to, small meetings, LinkedIn, and the rows of their modules
that name someone); this module is the person's way of keeping them.

- **Circles:** the person's own groups, each with who belongs in it in their words, whether they
  keep it warm and how often (Every (days)). Rename, change or remove any; add one in a sentence.
- **Members:** who is in which circle. Alpha proposes (State Proposed, with why) and the person
  says In or Left out, on the card or in the table; their word stands. A person can be in more
  than one circle. Ask to add someone to a circle and land them In.
- **Closeness:** for everyone in a circle, Close, In touch, Drifting, Quiet or No contact yet, by
  the rules in **Closeness rules** (the first by Order whose numbers they meet), with the rule and
  their numbers beside it. Never a score.
- **Follow-ups:** in the circles kept warm, someone with an address and no message or meeting for
  longer than the circle's days, once per quiet spell. Draft a note drafts the words in **Notes** for the person's
  yes ({first}, {company} filled in); Not now leaves it. Note says what became of it.
- **LinkedIn:** the LinkedIn connection (Settings › Connections) reads the person's connections
  into LinkedIn connections. When it can't (blocked, or not connected), LinkedIn's own export
  (Connections.csv) dropped in is read into LinkedIn here. Either way each connection is one of
  their people, at their company, by their profile link.
