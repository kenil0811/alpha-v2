# Network

Who the person knows. The people themselves are Alpha's own (view `people`: from Contacts, the
people they write to, small meetings, LinkedIn, and the rows of their modules that name someone);
Network is where they see them: Overview, People and Organizations, nothing else.

- **People's lists:** Clients, Insurers, My team and the like come from the tables whose rows are
  people (a table that names people by a role's address puts them in that role's list); My
  contacts holds the rest. Network keeps no groups of its own.
- **LinkedIn:** the LinkedIn connection (Settings › Connections) reads the person's connections
  into LinkedIn connections. When it can't (blocked, or not connected), LinkedIn's own export
  (Connections.csv) dropped in is read into LinkedIn here. Either way each connection is one of
  their people, at their company, by their profile link.
