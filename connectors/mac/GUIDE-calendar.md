# Calendar

The person's own calendar on this Mac, read-only. Events from 90 days back to 60 days ahead are
kept in the Events table (view `events`), with their attendees and organizer as "Name <email>"; the people at a small meeting are also
the person's people (view `people`). For a time
outside that, or the latest before a meeting, ask the calendar itself for the events between two
times. Alpha never adds, moves or answers an event.
