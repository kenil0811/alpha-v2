# 26 October 2026: serif kept to titles and big numbers

The agreed rule (25 Oct): serif only for the hero heading, a page's or a record's title and big
numbers. `look.test.ts` now holds it: `font-serif` passes on an h1, at 2xl, on tabular figures, or a
reply's own top heading, and fails anywhere else. Three places broke it and are sans now: an agent
page's Runs heading, a pop-up's title, and second-level headings in Alpha's replies. Alpha's
one-letter mark is a named exception. `table/*` is skipped until the other session's table PRs
land (its chart, calendar and form view headings are serif); then the skip goes.
