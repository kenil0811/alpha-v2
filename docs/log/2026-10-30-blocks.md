# 30 October 2026: blocks move and resize from their corner; hover layers on top

Design only, for Kenil's review; from the 27–30 Oct review.

- `arrange.tsx`: a page's blocks in rows. A handle on hover drops a block beside, above or below
  another (arrows do it from the keyboard); the bottom-right corner resizes it, the ↘↖ cursor its
  only mark, kept as "key@40x320"; a double-click gives the size back. On Home, a module's Agents
  and notes, an agent's page and Connections' tiles.
- `table/Blocks.tsx`: a row's notes on its own page as open blocks ("/" for a kind, Markdown
  starts, to-dos, drag to move), kept as Markdown, saved a moment after typing stops.
- One z scale in `app.css` (`--z-raised` 10 to `--z-tip` 60); menus and the tooltip portal into
  the open dialog (`floatRoot`), so a hover layer is never behind one.
- `stack.tsx` (cards of one kind as one card, 1 of N, one answer for all) ships unused, for Needs
  you's slice.
- Waits on the core: the arrangement lasts while the window is open. The design keeps it per
  person (§14); that needs an `arrange` field on `Layout` and its route, then `arrange.tsx` reads
  `useLayout` instead of its map.
