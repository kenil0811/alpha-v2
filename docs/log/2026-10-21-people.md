# 21 October 2026: people, part 1 (the store, its feeds, likely matches, the screens)

Built on Kenil's yes to "Plan: People and the Network kit" (part 1), with his answer to its
question: from mail, only those he writes to become people.

- **The store** (world/people.ts, migration 10): people and companies, each key (email, E.164
  phone, profile link, domain, company name) the one's whose row first gave it, what each row said
  of someone (people_sources), who works where with start and end (people_links), pairs that may
  be one (people_pairs), and every message or meeting by address (exchanges). People are derived:
  a table says how its rows name people (tables.people: each row a person, or an exchange with
  people), and every land, add, change and removal of its rows reads them again in the same
  transaction. A row naming two holders notes the pair and merges nothing; a contact's new address
  stays on the same person and the old one is let go; a removed row takes only what it alone
  brought; the person's own name for someone is a source of its own and outlives the rows. Merge
  keeps both rows (the merged one points at the other); Unmerge gives one back its keys and rows
  and keeps the two apart. Phones read with libphonenumber-js (1.13.15, max metadata), a number
  without its code in the country most kept numbers are in, else the Mac's (v1's rule).
- **Feeds:** Contacts (now with title, LinkedIn link and the person's own card, which the helper
  marks and which is never one of their people), Calendar (the attendee and organizer the Mac says
  is the person; the organizer says who asked for a meeting) and Gmail. From mail only recipients
  of what the person sends become people; every message to or from an address counts for whoever
  holds it. Meetings of more than 20 people (config/people.yaml, with the mail services that make
  no company) make no one. The person's addresses: their accounts, what they send from and what
  the Mac says.
- **Likely matches:** the same full name (two words or more), or the same name at the same
  company, found whenever people change and put on one waiting match card (Merge, Keep apart,
  Merge all with the same name and company). A merge that makes another pair one, or two pairs ask
  of the same two, withdraws the extra; a decided pair never comes back.
- **The window:** People in the rail (People · Companies, a find field, numbers over 90 days, the
  last contact and who wrote first), a page each (how to reach them, works at now and before,
  coming up, mail and meetings opening their rows, rows of the modules that name them, where Alpha
  knows them from, merged ones with Unmerge, a name of the person's own), companies with their
  people, the match card in Needs you, people in Find (⌘K). Home no longer hides a waiting card
  behind the welcome.
- **The model:** views people, companies, people_at and exchanges (people_at_lines and people_up
  under them), and a line in the start layer. A person field holds one of the person's people, by
  id, an address they hold or a name only one has; anything else is refused in words.
- **Kits:** module.yaml's `people:` on a table, mirrored back. DealPilot 0.4.1 (alpha-avilo): a
  source's contact at its firm, and a deal's broker by Broker email, are people.
- Tests: 163 (people.test.ts 18, a kit test). Scale on made-up data: 800 contacts in 0.4 s, 9,000
  messages in 11.5 s against 9.7 s for landing them alone (the landing itself, not people, is most
  of it), one new message in 16 ms, the list of 975 in 35 ms.
- Looked at on a made-up world (.data/people-check, core on 4720), light and dark, in WebKit.
  Kenil's own world has no Contacts, Calendar or Gmail connected since the 20 Oct wipe and no deal
  has a broker's address yet, so his people come when he connects them: his look is the
  acceptance.
- Found, not changed: a model query may name a common table expression after one of its views and
  so read the world's tables under it; the wall holds views only by name.
- **On Kenil's own data** (noon): he connected Gmail, Calendar and Contacts in the DealPilot test
  copy (4710), which still ran the code from before people. On it: 676 people, 26 companies, 14
  pairs, all of which he merged. What it showed, fixed: his own card has no address, so his other
  address was a person (a person's page now has "This is you": their addresses become his own and
  they leave People); subdomains made companies (alerts.sbi.co.in; a company is now its main
  domain, the second levels under a country's code in config/people.yaml); domain names read as
  words (Chase Global). Rules raised to 2: a world read by older rules reads every row again at
  start, keeping ids, merges and the person's word.
- **One Alpha** (Kenil: "keep one instance of alpha going forwards, for all testing and
  everything"): his own on 4700. The test copy and the scratch cores stopped; .data/restart.sh
  starts only his. His three connections moved into his world (the Gmail app password stays in
  the Keychain under the same name) and his 14 merges made again there by their keys: 662 people,
  25 companies, the match card answered "Merged 14, kept 0 apart".
