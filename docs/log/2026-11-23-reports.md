# 23 Nov: what you see is the PDF

Vikas asked for one report, the document itself, editable where it shows.

- A document page (`alpha-look` document) is drawn on its paper as the core prints it: A4
  (landscape when it prints so), 14 by 12 mm margins, laid out at the printed width (703 px), in
  the kit's print look, white, shrunk to the pane's width (CSS zoom). On a record's page it is its
  tab alone, no second title or frame. A long word breaks before it runs past its box.
- Page kit: text from an `open` naming a row and a field (headings too: `open`, `asideOpen`)
  edits by double-click; ✕ ✓ under it in YesNo's look; Enter saves, Escape cancels, leaving does
  not save; the bar is never printed. The window writes the edit to the field as the person's
  (`editedValue`: the whole value, a line of a list, or a figure in a line).
- A record with a page opens on it: its tab on the record's page, first in the side panel.

Not done: in the narrow side panel the fitted sheet is small (about a third of its size); a
document is one long sheet, not cut into the PDF's pages.
