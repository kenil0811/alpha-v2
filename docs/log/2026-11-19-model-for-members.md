# 19 Nov 2026: Settings › Model for members

Kenil asked that Settings › Model be there for people who are not the owner. A member now sees
Model with the owner's choices and help, and sets the route, a server's token or a key as the
owner does: the three model doors (`POST /api/model`, `POST` and `DELETE /api/model/token`) no
longer refuse a member, and each change is journaled as that person's. The model is one per
Alpha, so a member's change reaches everyone. Data, People and installing packs stay the owner's.

owner does, and the journal says it was them" holds it.
