# 21 October 2026: people, part 2: the Network module, Alpha's own

Part 1 committed and pushed on Kenil's "ok looks good.." (to 908ea5b), and DealPilot 0.4.1 put
in his Alpha. Then part 2 of "Plan: People and the Network kit", and what it needed of the
platform.

- **Rows put to the person** (modules/reviews.ts, ReviewCard): `alpha.review({ words, table,
  rows, field, choices, note })` opens one card in Needs you with each row, its why (a field) and
  the choices of one choice field; a choice is journaled as the person setting it and stated, so a
  later land never writes over it; "In: all n" decides the rest; the card is answered with the
  counts once every row has one. The journey runner gains `review: { choice, words }`.
- **The model sorting** (modules/judging.ts, config/judging.md): `alpha.judge({ groups, items })`,
  120 items a call, each answered with one group or none and why; a group or an item not asked is
  dropped. Its own instructions, the reading route's model; tokens counted on the run.
- **People for scripts:** `alpha.people.list(kind)`: each with addresses, phones, companies,
  titles (new in the list), contact numbers, since when Alpha knows them, and the rows of the
  person's modules that name them (People.namings, counted by table and field, in one pass).
- An agent's run is handed `lastRun`; `rows.list` gives each row's `updatedAt`.
- A row is named by the person a person field holds when that field comes first (world.rowName):
  on cards, drafts ("Send the Follow-up for Sam Ortiz to …"), Find and the rows naming someone.
- **The Network kit** (first written in alpha-avilo, moved here the same day): Circles (Name, Who
  belongs in the person's words, Keep warm, Every (days)), Members (Person, Circle, State Proposed
  / In / Left out, Why), Closeness (per person in a circle: the label, last contact, in 90 days, who
  wrote first), Closeness rules, Follow-ups (one per quiet spell: Answer, Note), Notes (the
  follow-up's words), LinkedIn (a people map: each connection a person by profile link). Agents:
  Circles (the model sorts people with more known than a name against each circle's words;
  everyone when a circle changes, else those met since; one card per circle), Closeness (labels by
  the rules, computed with the rule as formula and the numbers as note; follow-ups for people with
  an address quiet past a kept-warm circle's days, one card; notes drafted from Notes for the
  person's yes), LinkedIn export (Connections.csv by its header, each value citing its cell).
