# 21 October 2026: the Task manager (tasks, the Tasks screen, agents on tasks)

Built on Kenil's yes to "Plan: the Task manager", with the pick he took: only runs that serve a
task show on it; an agent's runs on its own trigger stay in Activity.

- **The store** (world/tasks.ts, migration 11): tasks with a state (To do, In progress, Waiting,
  Done, Dropped), a place (module, table, row, field or page, resolved by name or id, said in
  words), what else they touch (rows, people, files), due and reminder, a repeat in words kept as a
  rule (every day, weekday, Monday, 2 weeks, month, the 1st of each month, year), priority, owner
  (the person, Alpha, or an agent), parent, made by. Every change journaled. A reminder comes once
  at its time (scheduler, osascript notification, a line on Home); a repeating task makes its next
  once, when it is done or dropped or its day passed, never in the past. Near-duplicates: an open
  task on the same place with 60% of the same words.
- **Alpha's hands:** one `task` tool (add, change, start, wait, done, drop, reopen, remove, find);
  a task without a place sits on the module asked from; removing the person's own asks first. The
  model reads tasks in the view `tasks`; the start layer counts open, overdue and due today.
- **The Tasks screen:** Tasks in the rail with a count; saved lists with counts; list, by place,
  board (drag between states) and calendar; New task; a task's page (state, due, reminder, repeat,
  priority, owner, where with Take off, notes, checklist, where it sits with the row's values and
  other tasks there, what it touches, history, Remove). A row's panel lists its tasks with Add a
  task, and a row with open tasks shows their count beside its name. Home: Today's tasks. ⌘K: Tasks,
  New task.
- **Agents on tasks** (migration 12): an agent file with `tasks: true` takes the tasks given to it,
  and needs no trigger. Start (the task's page, or "start it" in words) runs it with
  `alpha.input.task` (the task, its place in words, the row's values by field name, what it
  touches, what earlier runs said and what came of their cards); the run carries the task.
  `alpha.task.done(words)` is the only way it becomes Done; `alpha.task.wait(words)` says what it
  waits for; a run that says neither leaves it Waiting, saying so; Stop puts it back in To do; a run
  whose cards wait leaves it Waiting for your yes, and the agent runs on it again once every card
  is decided. `alpha.task.propose([...])` puts tasks on one card, each ticked; the kept ones are
  added on the yes, made by the agent. A task's page shows its agent with Start, the run with its
  steps and Stop, earlier runs, and its approvals; lists show "working" and "Waiting for your yes".
  The owner list offers only agents that take tasks.
