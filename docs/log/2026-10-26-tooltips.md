# 26 October 2026: one tooltip

Every tooltip was the browser's own `title`: unstyled, late, sometimes missing. `ui/tooltip.tsx`
is now the one: a small ink-on-surface label (dark mode from the tokens) under anything with a
`data-tip`, 150ms after the pointer rests or at once on keyboard focus, placed against the window
so nothing clips it, the next one at once while one shows. `IconButton` sets it from its `label`
(still the `aria-label`); the raw icon-only buttons switched one by one (the bell, the folded
assistant's face, the rail's fold and folded-strip icons, a row's ⋮ and open, a column's menu, Add
a field, the table's ⋮ and numbers chevron, Send, Change the name, the companion's close). A
disabled menu line, the sort editor's Add sort and a table's New show their reason the same way.
`look.test.ts` now fails on an `IconButton` without a label. The pencil is not on this branch; it
should use `IconButton` (or `data-tip`) when it lands. Checked in Edge by hover and Tab, light and
dark: the bell, Fold the assistant, a disabled Send, the folded rail's Home and Open the rail.
