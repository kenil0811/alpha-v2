# 26 October 2026: a module's menu in the rail

A module's ⋮ menu in the rail, also on a right-click or Shift+F10, now reads Open, Rename, Change
icon ›, Move to ›, Hide, and Hidden modules › when any are hidden. A right-click on the rail's
empty space brings hidden ones back too. The core has no route to rename a module or keep its icon,
so those two lines are greyed and say why on hover; the request is in the PR for Kenil. Hide is kept
in this browser only (the core has no hidden flag); the last module in the rail can't be hidden,
and a hidden one still opens by its link and by Find. Checked in the browser on the test install,
by mouse and keyboard: Alpha hidden, Network's Hide greyed, Alpha back from the empty-space menu.
