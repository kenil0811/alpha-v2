# 26 October 2026: undo, core step 1

The first step of the core plan (Kenil's yes, all six calls as recommended). A removed row now
leaves its table and waits whole in `removed_rows` for `keep_removed_days` (30, limits.yaml): its
values, provenance, versions, files, kit row and the links both ways. Each version keeps the line
that replaced it and what that line wrote (migration 21, which also marks `meta.undo_since`), so a
run, a turn or one line can be reverted: added rows to the trash, written values back where they
are still what was written, removed rows back. The revert is a line of its own, revertible; a row
a revert brought back and a later revert takes away goes back under its own removal. Activity
lists Alpha's turns that changed rows, removals and reverts beside the runs, with Revert or Bring
back behind ⋮ and the one pop-up, whose words come from the core running the same work and
rolling it back. The scheduler's tick deletes trash past its days with a line; a table or field
removed empties its trash; the archive leaves it out. A revert of an agent's run does not wake
that agent; Alpha hears of reverts since its last turn. The tests caught two things the first
draft got wrong: a link cleared by a removal read as the run's own change (so each version now
keeps what its line wrote, not a diff against the next version), and a revert of a revert left a
row under the wrong removal. The journeys runner's checks moved to `journeys/checks.ts` (the file
was at the 600-line wall) and journeys gained a `revert` step; `revert-a-run` is the new journey.
