# 26 October 2026: header tabs never cut a name short

The pill switch in the bar shows every tab's whole name, or, when the names don't all fit, icons
only, each naming itself on hover. It measures: every change of the bar's width tries the names
again. Before, tabs were cut to "P…" and "C..". Checked in Edge: Intelligence shows icons at 800,
1000 and 1440 with the assistant open and its names at 1900; Network's ten tabs show icons at 1440.
