(migration 22) holds every allow and deny, approved by the person or inferred by Alpha, scoped to
all of Alpha, a module or an agent, covering a call in parts code matches: does (read, change,
remove, send, reach, sign in), through, to (inside, outside, addresses), sites, tables. Its words
