# 23 Nov: Home, one place for what needs you (PR 1)

Rebuilt from the 22–23 Nov reference, on PR 0's standard card.

- Home's cards sit on a 48-column grid (`CardGrid`), each one `Frame`, at 1, 1/2, 1/3 or 1/4, or a
  square of 1/8 or 1/16 (icon and number); dragged by the right edge or ← →; the core keeps each
  person's widths (`cardWidth`, one preference per card). A card shows only when it has something.
- Needs you is one card: a face per agent with something waiting, Schedules (a clock: what a
  schedule's run raised, which the core now names on each card) and Sign-ins (a key), each with its
  count; one thing at a time, the table's page numbers under it. A card inside shows its body only.
- A question about several things lists them with boxes, one line each, the rest on hover; a partial
  yes answers "<yes>: only <names>". ✓ and ✕ (`YesNo`); own words behind a chat icon, in the same row
  as the page numbers.
- A microphone (`ui/voice.tsx`) in Home's ask box, the composer and a question's own-answer box.
- Set up Alpha is a card (steps done, a bar, Next, Carry on, Decline), and leaves the workspace
  tile's menu; Activity and its count leave the tile too.
- The date and greeting are in Home's bar, with no ‹ and no name. A long word breaks in its box.
