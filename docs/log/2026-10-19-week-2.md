## What was built (uncommitted, for Kenil's review)

- **The world, migration 2:** a `connections` table (connector, account, state, health, a
  reference to its secret and never the secret, a cursor, the last sync); rows a connection lands
  keep the item's own id (`records.item_key`), so the next sync changes them in place with a
  version; a card's `detail` (the message as it will go). A world at version 1 is backed up
  before it moves.
- **The connector SDK** (`connector-sdk/`): a connector is a manifest, the tables its items land
  in, tools whose effects the gates read, a check and a sync. Every call is paced per account
  (`limiter`, a daily budget where a service has one), retried when it failed in passing,
  journaled, and recorded or replayed from fixtures; a sync ends in a health judgement ("fewer
  rows than last time", "nothing new for N days"). Secrets live in the Keychain through Alpha's
  helper (`connector-sdk/helper/`, Swift, rebuilt only when its source changes).
- **Connectors:** Gmail (`connectors/gmail`: search with Gmail's own words, read a message, save
  a draft as a write, send as outward with the whole message on its card, 90 days of headers
  synced into Mail by Message-ID); Calendar and Contacts (`connectors/mac`, read-only, through the
  helper app); watched folders (`connectors/files`: what is new or changed is kept and read as if
  dropped in; the reader moved here from core).
- **The core:** `ConnectorService` (connect, disconnect, sync, one at a time per connection;
  every known connector's tools always served on a second in-process server so the desk's tools
  never change, loaded through tool search, each call's effect through the gates); a `connect`
  world tool whose effect is the sign-in gate (its yes opens the form in Settings); the scheduler,
  one tick a minute, journaling a sleep and catching up once; the start layer lists the
  connections with their guides; granted folders are the watched-folder connections, read-only in
  the sandbox.
- **The window:** Settings with Connections (each connector's form: the account, the app
  password with Google's help link, "Read now", "Disconnect") and Folders; a card shows its full
  message.

## Mail, kept fuller and out of the sidebar

Kenil asked why mail is kept as a table (answer: questions across sources, Network's last contact
and frequency, noticing, sources and speed; the copy is headers only). His calls: keep it, add
more of each message's metadata (not its text), and hide it by default.

- Gmail now keeps, per message: direction (sent or received), each to and cc address on its own,
  the attachments' names (from the message's structure, nothing downloaded), unread and starred.
  The cursor carries the shape (`v2`), so the 90 days are read again in the new shape; unread and
  starred follow changes since the last read through IMAP's CONDSTORE, changing only rows already
  kept (`land(..., { existing: true })`).
- Migration 3: `tables.hidden`. Tables a connector makes are out of the sidebar by default (Mail,
  and Events and Contacts by the same reasoning); Settings › Connections names the table each
  connection keeps, with "Show in the sidebar". The model reads them all the same.
- Ran: 48 tests; `mail-on-recorded-answers` passed again (42 s). Kenil's own Alpha restarted (his
  world backed up as v2 first): his three connections' tables are out of the sidebar, and his
  Gmail was read again in the new shape: 628 messages, 57 sent, 106 with attachments, 8 unread.
