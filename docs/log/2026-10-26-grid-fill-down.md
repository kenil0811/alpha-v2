# 26 October 2026: ⇧↓ ranges and ⌘D in the grid

The paste from a spreadsheet was already there (a block from the picked cell across and down, new
rows past the end, a yes first); it now also takes one copied value into every row of a range or
ticked under the picked cell. ⇧↓ and ⇧↑ stretch the picked cell down its column, shaded. ⌘D (Ctrl+D)
fills the picked value down over that range, or over the ticked rows below it, through the same
bar, which reads Fill instead of Paste, and the same `/rows/many`. Checked in Edge on Circles with
the write stopped in the browser: range, ⌘D over a range and over ticked rows, a one-value and a
block paste.
