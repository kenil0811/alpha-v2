- **alpha-v2, pages (design §13 made real):** `core/src/pages/`. A page is `pages/<name>.html`
  in a module; `pagesOf` lists them by `<title>`, `<meta name="alpha-front">` first. The core
  builds the frame's document with Alpha's head first (a content policy that lets nothing out,
  the window's fonts carried inside, the kit's look and the runtime), then the page's file
  without its own html, head and body tags. `frame/runtime.js` is `alpha`: `rows` and `history`
  of the page's own module (read-only, answered through the window by message, or by the core
  when printing), `input` choosers, `open`, `page`. `frame/kit.js` is the blocks (heading, text,
  stats with change, chart, table with cells that carry a tone and what they open, list, card,
  grid), rewritten from v1's page kit in the Ledger look. The window shows a page as a module tab
  and in the rail, sends it the look in light and dark, sizes the frame to it, opens rows it asks
  for (`#/table/<id>?only=…`, with Show all) and downloads it as a PDF. The SDK's `renderPage`
  prints a page with the network shut, at the paper's width, landscape when the page says so.
  `look` with a module and page gives the model a picture of the page; the `keep` tool says what
  a page is. A journey check `page` matches a page's drawn words.
