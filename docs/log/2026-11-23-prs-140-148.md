Taken with the review's fix. One motion set: three speeds and two curves (`ease-spring`,
`ease-soft`) as tokens in `app.css`, folded together with batch 1's feedback set (one `press`,
`lift`, `pop` and `rise-in`, timed on them; one `pop` keyframe). What an agent is doing said in
words with a soft shimmer instead of spinners; buttons and switches give, tick boxes pop, streamed
words fade in; the face's moods (listening, thinking, done, could not finish); a row's panel grows
out of its row and screens cross-fade; a finished task's tick draws, its line strikes through and

The fix: the PRs saved a finished task and sent a card's answer only after the animation, so a
failed save was swallowed and closing the window then lost it. Now both go the moment they are
made (`leaveWith` in ui/motion.ts): the thing is held while it animates out, lists drawn through
`useLeaving` (Home's cards and tasks, the conversation's cards, task lists) keep drawing it where it
was whatever a live update says, and a failure brings it back with its problem. Timed in WebKit:
the save's request leaves 63 ms after the click. Rules L23 to L26 in ui-rules.md, L25 held by
ui/motion.test.ts. A task's line moved to TaskLine.tsx and the face's mood to mood.ts, so Tasks
and App stay inside their walls.
