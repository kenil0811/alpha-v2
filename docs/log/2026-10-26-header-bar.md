# 26 October 2026: the header bar without Find or a squeezed way back

The bar over each screen no longer holds the "Find anything ⌘K" box; ⌘K still opens Find from any
screen, and the `onFind` prop is gone. Inside a module (its pages and its tables) the bar shows no
way back, since the module's tabs are the way around; elsewhere the way back shows its whole name
or, when it cannot fit beside the middle switch, not at all. Checked in Edge at 1440×900 on Home,
People › Organizations, Tasks and a module's table, and with a stretched label that drops away.
