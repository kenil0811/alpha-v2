# 23 Nov: tables read at a glance

Vikas asked that a table read like a spreadsheet: lines that run to the foot, a line after every
header, long words that spill into empty cells, the month on one screen, a tidier view menu.

- `table/below.tsx`: the room under the last row is ruled at a row's height, so the row lines run
  to the foot however few the rows. Every column's head ends in a line, not only the last frozen one.
- A column shows words longer than it is wide as Overflow (the default: over the empty cells after
  it, as in Sheets), Wrap or Clip, picked under Long text on its header's right-click. The core
  keeps `clip` beside `wrap` on a view's columns (module.yaml by name, dropped with its field).
  `layout.ts` `runOn` works out one row's overflow; a frozen column never overflows, and the cells
  scrolling by pass under it.
- The calendar's month fits all its weeks in the frame with no scroll; a day shows the rows its
  height holds and "n more".
- The view menu: the ✓ after the name, no wider than its words, under the view button's left edge.
  `MenuItem`'s `tickAfter` is opt-in, so other menus are unchanged.

Not done: the model's description of a saved view (`engine/tools/common.ts`) does not name `clip`
yet; it is words for the model in code, to move to `core/config/` first. An empty cell that an
overflow could reach loses its dash even when the words before it fit.
